Book a tricycle anywhere in Balayan

PASADA connects student commuters with registered tricycle drivers in Balayan, Batangas. See the fare and travel time before you book, and watch your tricycle arrive on the map.

How to install

  1. Download the app on your Android phone

    Tap Download for Android. Google Drive says it can't scan the file for viruses because it's large; tap Download anyway. When it finishes, open PASADA app.apk from the notification or from Files → Downloads.

  2. Allow installing from this source

    If Android says it can't install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back. You only do this once.

  3. Tap Install

    If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unrecognized, tap More details then Install anyway. It shows this for every app that isn't from the Play Store.

  4. Open PASADA and create your account

    Choose Student or Driver. Allow notifications and location when asked, so you get booking updates and can use your current location.

PASADA updates itself. New fixes download automatically when you open the app, so there's no need to reinstall.

What your phone needs

  • Android 7.0 or newer (Android 9+ with 3 GB RAM runs best)
  • Mobile data or Wi-Fi while you book and ride
  • Location (GPS) turned on
  • Google Play services, for booking notifications
  • About 150 MB of free storage

Not available for iPhone.

Before you start

Balayan, Batangas only. Pickup and destination must both be inside Balayan.

Drivers need approval. Upload a clear photo of your driver's license when you register. You can go online once it's verified.

Pay the driver directly. The fare shown is an estimate; PASADA doesn't handle payments.
